i
Technical boundary: ML-KEM-768 and AES-256-GCM are genuinely executed locally in this browser. The origin signature in this learning prototype uses browser ECDSA P-256 so the signature flow is real and inspectable; ECDSA is not post-quantum secure. A production defence deployment should use a standardised hybrid authentication design and migrate the signature layer to ML-DSA, with hardware-backed keys, audited identities, secure transport, key rotation, and independent review.

      


      

        
WHY THIS SHOWS THE QUANTUM PROBLEM

        
Defence communication needs more than encryption.

        

          
CONFIDENTIALITY
Quantum-resistant key establishment
ML-KEM replaces the RSA/ECC-style exchange that a future quantum computer could target with Shor’s algorithm.

          
INTEGRITY
Any modification is rejected
The signed packet and AES-GCM authentication make changed ciphertext fail verification before it can be trusted.

          
FRESHNESS
Old instructions cannot be replayed
A unique packet ID, expiry time, and receiver-side replay registry stop a valid old packet from being accepted twice.
