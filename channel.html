HOW THIS SOLVES THE PROBLEM

        
Replace the vulnerable key-establishment layer.

        

          
01
Before
Many systems use RSA/ECC to establish or protect a symmetric encryption key. A future large quantum computer threatens that public-key layer.

          
02
QuantumShield channel
ML-KEM establishes the AES session key without relying on RSA factoring or ECC discrete-log assumptions.

          
03
What scales
A relay server can carry encrypted packets without accessing plaintext. Organisations can migrate application by application.
