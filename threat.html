WHY THIS MATTERS

The danger is not “quantum reads every ciphertext.”

It is that an attacker could use Shor’s algorithm to undermine RSA/ECC key exchange or digital signatures, and thereby obtain keys or impersonate trusted systems. AES-256 remains a strong choice for message encryption.

So what should organisations do?
